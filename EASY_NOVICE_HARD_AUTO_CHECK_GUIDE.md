# 🎯 Quick Guide: Automatic Checking for All Challenges

## ✨ What's New?

**Submit button removed!** All challenges now check automatically.

---

## 🎮 How It Works

### Step 1: Select a Challenge
- Choose Easy, Novice, Medium, or Hard difficulty
- Select a specific challenge scenario

### Step 2: Build Your Solution
- Add devices (PC, Router, Switch)
- Create connections (Wired/Wireless)
- Configure IP addresses
- Arrange your topology

### Step 3: Wait for Auto-Check
- **Pause for 1.5 seconds**
- Auto-check runs automatically
- Results popup appears with feedback

### Step 4: Iterate
- Make adjustments based on feedback
- Auto-check runs again after changes
- Repeat until complete! ✅

---

## ⏱️ Auto-Check Timing

| Action | Auto-Check Delay |
|--------|-----------------|
| Add Device | 1.5 seconds |
| Create Connection | 1.5 seconds |
| Configure IP | 1.5 seconds |
| Delete Device/Connection | 1.5 seconds |

**Why 1.5 seconds?**
- Gives you time to make multiple changes
- Prevents checking too often
- Feels instant once you pause

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Do I need to click anything to submit?**  
A: No! Just wait 1.5 seconds after your last change.

**Q: What if I keep making changes?**  
A: Timer resets with each action. Auto-check runs after you stop.

**Q: Can I force an immediate check?**  
A: Not currently, but just wait 1.5 seconds - it's super fast!

**Q: Does this work in multiplayer mode?**  
A: Yes! Auto-check works in both solo and multiplayer challenges.

**Q: What about Foundation challenges?**  
A: They complete instantly when you meet all objectives.
